  The Gauss-Codazzi formalism is used to obtain exact solutions to Einstein's equations in the pres-
ence of domain walls. Domain walls are shown to have repulsive gravitational fields. The most
general solution to Einstein's equations for a planar domain wall is obtained. Also, the motion of a
spherical domain wall in an asymptotically flat space-timeis derived.
