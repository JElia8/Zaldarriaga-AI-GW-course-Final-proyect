Regular Wave Solutions of the Interior Regge-Wheeler and Zerilli

                                 Equations

                                      Inside a Thin Spherical Shell

Setup: a thin spherical shell of matter sits at areal radius r = R. Outside (r > R) the spacetime
is Schwarzschild with total mass M ; inside (r < R) it is vacuum. We solve the master (Regge-
Wheeler/Zerilli) equations of Martel & Poisson (2005) [1] for a general perturbation of this interior
region, in the frequency domain, imposing regularity at r = 0.
